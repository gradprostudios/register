Slot Settings

Set each school's booking window, then generate its time slots. Students only see slots for their own school, and only while booking is enabled. Times are Philippine time (Asia/Manila).

to

Leave "to" empty for a single day. Blocked days get no slots, and existing slots on those days are closed when you save.

—

Every slot is 1 hour (e.g. 8–9 AM, 9–10 AM). The total is divided across all the hours in the schedule; saving also updates the number of students per hour on existing upcoming slots. Generate adds slots that don't exist yet. Rebuild removes upcoming slots nobody has booked and makes them again from these settings (use it after changing hours or days); booked students are never removed.
